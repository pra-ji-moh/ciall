    About it

    Its brain is the being's own C code (brain/ob_life.c), compiled to run here unchanged. Its brainstem is restless of itself and fires when its own activity crosses threshold. Its basal ganglia lean on that, learning from dopamine that is its learning progress, not surprise. Plain surprise would trap it in front of anything random; progress lets it get bored and move on.

    Everything stays on this phone. Pictures, sounds and touches go only into its brain, here in the page. Nothing is uploaded. It remembers you between visits on this device only.

    Inside Claude, pages may not use the live camera or microphone. The upload button opens your phone's camera instead. To give it live senses, open this same page from its own web address (phone/index.html in the repository, served over https).
