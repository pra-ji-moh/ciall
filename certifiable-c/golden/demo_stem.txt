  roots, each PROVED to exist and to be the only one in its interval:
    in [0.9999999998, 1.0000000003]
    in [1.9999999995, 2.0000000001]
    in [2.9999999999, 3.0000000005]

  where it turns (roots of the derivative): 1.422650 2.577350

WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO YET
