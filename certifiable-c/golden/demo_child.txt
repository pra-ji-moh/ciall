WHAT HAPPENED
  It chose every experiment itself, always where it was blind or where
  it had guessed and could be caught out. It gained counting, then a
  rule for the scale, then pouring, then a law reaching past anything it
  had poured, then a theorem about every number. It remembered all of it
  from one day to the next. And when the world stopped agreeing with its
  law, it did not explain the result away: it lost the ability that
  rested on it and said so.

  What is still missing: it cannot make up a NEW KIND of experiment,
  only choose among the ones its world offers. Inventing the question
  "what if I took pebbles away?" is the next thing to build.
