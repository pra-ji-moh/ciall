An autonomous learner, running on smarsh_learner.c.

No curriculum. Nothing tells it what to acquire or when. It is given goals
and an environment, and it works out the rest:

    cannot even PHRASE the goal    -> acquire the smallest capacity that
                                      would let it, and try again
    can phrase it, cannot settle   -> work out which single measurement
                                      would settle it, and take that one
    settled                        -> answer, with a witness
    the evidence contradicts       -> find what it was told that cannot be
                                      true, drop it, carry on

Every one of those decisions is counted, not scored. The only preference
in the whole loop is which capacity to acquire when several would do, and
it is named as a preference rather than buried.

==========================================================================
goal                      worlds  acquired  measured     outcome  proved
==========================================================================
is a bigger than b             3         1         1 derived = 1     yes
      acquire more     worlds 1 -> 3
      measure more
are they within one            5         1         1 derived = 1     yes
      acquire close    worlds 3 -> 5
      measure close
is it called a cup            10         1         1 derived = 1     yes
      acquire name     worlds 5 -> 10
      measure name
is a at least two             28         1         0 derived = 1     yes
      acquire exact_a  worlds 10 -> 28
is the total four             32         1         1 derived = 1     yes
      acquire sum      worlds 28 -> 32
      measure sum
is the total even             32         0         0 derived = 1     yes

  it now holds: more, close, name, exact_a, sum
  total acquisitions 5, total measurements 4

  Nobody told it the order. Each capacity was acquired at the moment
  a goal could not be phrased without it, and never before.

THE SAME EVIDENCE, A FINER VOCABULARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  glance recorded: [('more', 2), ('close', 0), ('name', 1)]
  with 3 capacities (10 worlds): cannot even phrase it
  after acquiring exact_a (28 worlds), with NO new measurement: derived = 1

  It never counted the pile. It learned to ask about counts, replayed
  the glance it already had, and the answer was sitting in it. The
  vocabulary was the missing part, not the evidence.

BEING MISLED, AND GETTING OUT OF IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  told, after counting 3 and 1, that the first pile is the smaller one
  state: contradiction, 0 worlds standing
  after recovery: dropped more=0, 1 worlds standing, the total is four: derived = 1

  It did not lower a confidence and carry on. The claims were jointly
  impossible, which is a verdict of its own, and it dropped one and
  said which. Choosing the most recent is a policy; that the set was
  impossible is not.
