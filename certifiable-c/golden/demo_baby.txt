BABY -> RESEARCHER.

A program that starts unable to tell any two situations apart, and grows
by acquiring questions. Nothing here is scripted as "now it understands
arithmetic": every capacity has to EARN its place by refining the
partition, and a candidate that refines nothing is rejected as a
restatement however sophisticated it looks.

Runs on smarsh_reason.c, the code test_smarsh_reason.c checks.

the true situation: a=3, b=1, called a cup  (situation 27 of 32)

stage                 worlds  can ask  derived  bits unheld
------------------------------------------------------------
newborn                    1        0/6       0/6         0.00
comparison                 3        1/6       1/6         0.00
nearness                   5        1/6       1/6         0.00
naming                    10        2/6       2/6         0.00
counting                  32        6/6       3/6         3.58

It starts unable to tell any two situations apart, so it can form no
question at all, and it says so rather than producing an answer.

EARNING A CAPACITY
------------------------------------------------------------
  after comparison, it can tell 3 kinds of situation apart
  at_least   reject   splits nothing; it is what it already knows, said again
  close      ACQUIRE  splits 3 worlds into 5
  exact_a    ACQUIRE  splits 3 worlds into 10

  The rejection is not a judgement about sophistication. "at_least"
  is a perfectly good question. It is simply already definable, so
  acquiring it would add vocabulary and no capacity.

WHAT THE ORDER OF THE LADDER IS, AND IS NOT
------------------------------------------------------------
  most refining single capacity: sum (7 worlds in one step)
  the one it actually takes first: more (3 worlds)

  So the ladder is NOT ordered by how much a step teaches. Greedy
  would skip straight to exact counting. What orders it is what can
  be FORMED yet: you cannot ask how many before you can tell apart.
  That is a constructibility order, and it is not derivable from
  information alone. Saying otherwise would be dressing up a
  developmental fact as a theorem.

ARITHMETIC DOES NOT EXTEND STRUCTURE, IT SUBSUMES IT
------------------------------------------------------------
  structure alone        5 worlds
  exact counts alone    16 worlds
  can structure be recovered from counts?  yes
  can counts be recovered from structure?  no

  Once it can say "three", "more" was always derivable and stops
  being a separate thing it knows. The earlier stage is not thrown
  away, it becomes shorthand. That is checked, not asserted: the
  projection succeeds one way and refuses the other.

THE RESEARCHER: AN ANSWER THAT CARRIES ITS OWN PROOF
------------------------------------------------------------
  claim: the total is four
  from a glance alone: cannot say, 3 worlds still standing, 1.00 bits it does not hold

  It can phrase the question now and it still will not answer it.
  So it works out what to measure. Not by ranking the measurements
  as interesting: by counting what each one would leave standing.

    count the first pile    narrows it, but may not finish
    count the second pile   SETTLES IT whichever way it comes back
    count them together     SETTLES IT whichever way it comes back

  it chooses: count the second pile
  measures, and the total is four: derived, value 1, S = 0.500
  worlds still standing: 1 of 32
  a checker sharing none of the engine re-derives it from the witness alone: yes

  At the first stage it could not form this question. In the middle
  it could phrase it and refused to answer. At the end it worked out
  which single measurement would settle it, took that one, answered,
  and handed over a proof a stranger can check.

  Nothing in between was a confidence rising. Every step was worlds
  being ruled out, and the answer arrived when the survivors agreed.
