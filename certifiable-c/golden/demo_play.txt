THE CHILD AT PLAY
=================
Frames of 64 by 64 coloured cells, numbered actions, and a signal
when a level ends. Nothing else reaches it.

ONE. A maze world.
  finished every level after 70 actions
  itself: 4 cells that moved together, first seen after 2 actions, colours 3 6
  action 1: moves it -2 rows, +0 columns (held 13 times, blocked 2)
  action 2: moves it +2 rows, +0 columns (held 15 times)
  action 3: moves it +0 rows, -2 columns (held 14 times, blocked 1)
  action 4: moves it +0 rows, +2 columns (held 22 times)
  what blocks: colour 5  (from 1 failed moves)
  what ends a level: covering colour 4  (over 3 endings)
  level 1: 22 actions, shortest possible 15
  level 2: 31 actions, shortest possible 31
  level 3: 17 actions, shortest possible 17
  all levels: 70 actions against a shortest possible 63

TWO. The same mazes, actions shuffled, one action dead, a new ending colour.
  finished every level after 72 actions
  itself: 4 cells that moved together, first seen after 1 action, colours 3 6
  action 1: moves it +0 rows, +2 columns (held 22 times)
  action 2: moves it -2 rows, +0 columns (held 13 times, blocked 2)
  action 3: does nothing it could see (3 tries)
  action 4: moves it +0 rows, -2 columns (held 14 times)
  action 5: moves it +2 rows, +0 columns (held 15 times)
  what blocks: colour 5  (from 1 failed moves)
  what ends a level: covering colour 11  (over 3 endings)
  level 1: 24 actions, shortest possible 15
  level 2: 31 actions, shortest possible 31
  level 3: 17 actions, shortest possible 17
  all levels: 72 actions against a shortest possible 63
