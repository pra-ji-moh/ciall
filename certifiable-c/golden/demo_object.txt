THINGS, AND SAYING SOMETHING ABOUT ALL OF THEM
=============================================
Nothing below is a row of numbers. Each is a collection of things
with joins between them, and nothing is said about what they are.

ONE. Sets of numbers, and a count nobody explains.
  6 scenes, made of things and joins, with nothing else said
  evens is how many x with ((0 == (x.value % 2)))
  1399 descriptions were possible, 5 survived, support 0.9964
  another account fits just as well: how many x with ((how many joins == (x.value % 2)))
  the longest description it got to write was 5 steps of the 9 it
  allows, so nothing longer was searched, and nothing longer is claimed
  it had to invent the idea of counting the things a condition
  holds of, and the condition, before it could say that
