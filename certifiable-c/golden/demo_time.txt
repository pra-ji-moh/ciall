TIME AND CHANGE: A TOY CAR ON A TRACK
States: place 0 to 4, blue (b) or red (r). Pushes: left, right; walls at
both ends. It learns by watching pushes with the lights on.

WHAT IS REALLY DIFFERENT
  10 states as described; 5 that pushing and touching could ever tell apart.
  Colour never changes what a push does or what the sensor feels, so no
  amount of acting in the dark could reveal it. Time decides what matters.

IN THE DARK. It has no idea where the car is.
  at the start, possible:            {b0 b1 b2 b3 b4 r0 r1 r2 r3 r4}
  touch: not against a wall          {b1 b2 b3 r1 r2 r3}
  push right                         {b2 b3 b4 r2 r3 r4}
  touch: against a wall              {b4 r4}
  It knows exactly where the car is, and it knows it cannot know the colour.

ACTING WITHOUT KNOWING
  get the car to place 0, starting with no idea where it is:
    plan: left left left left   (12 sets of possibilities searched; re-checked by running it: works from every possible start)
  get a RED car to place 0, starting with no idea:
    NO PLAN EXISTS. All 15 sets of possibilities it could reach were tried.
  Pushing never changes the colour, and it proved that no sequence of
  pushes, of any length, can make sure of a red car.

A PUSH IT NEVER WATCHED
