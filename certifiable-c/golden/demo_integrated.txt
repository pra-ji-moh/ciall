A CONCEPT IT NAMES FOR ITSELF
  it defines: filled = s1 + s2 + s3, and can now speak of it

LEARNING A RULE: THE RULES THAT CONTRADICT WHAT IT SAW ARE ELIMINATED
  a bell rings when enough slots are filled, and it does not know how many
  k                                              = 2   (proved, standing on nothing it guessed)
  the threshold was not fitted or guessed: every other value was ruled out

A PROPERTY OF THAT RULE, PROVED IN THE SAME ENGINE
  filled >= k => filled + 1 >= k                 true in every world   (proved, standing on nothing it guessed)
  once the bell rings, adding a pebble never silences it: not tested, proved

  total > 50                                     true in every world   (resting on what it guessed: a claim of its own law;)

TIME: THE SAME QUANTITIES AT t, t+1, t+2, TIED BY CONSTRAINTS
  a car on a track of five places, walls at both ends, pushed twice
  p2 == 4                                        both are possible   (not proved either way)
      a world where it does, pushes and all: p0=2 p1=3 p2=4 a1=1 a2=1
  that witness IS the plan: no separate planner, just a world that fits
  p2 >= 1                                        both are possible   (not proved either way)
  p2 <= 4                                        true in every world   (proved, standing on nothing it guessed)
  abs(p2 - p0) <= 2                              true in every world   (proved, standing on nothing it guessed)
  whatever it does, it cannot move more than two places in two pushes:
  proved, not searched for and not tested

WHY THIS IS NOT LAYERS
  The concept, the rule it learned, the proof about that rule, the guess,
  the book it was told, the plan and the safety property are all the same
  kind of thing: constraints on a described world. One operation runs on
  all of them: eliminate what cannot be. Take any piece out and the rest
  still works, because there is no stack to fall over.
