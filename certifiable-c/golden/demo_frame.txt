FINDING THE FORMULATION
=======================
Four worlds, given as bare numbers. It is told nothing else.

ONE. Three trays of pebbles, and how many there are altogether.
  it was given 3 readings and 9 situations, and told nothing else
    t1       matters: situations 0 and 1 differ in it alone, and disagree
    t2       matters: situations 0 and 2 differ in it alone, and disagree
    t3       matters: situations 0 and 3 differ in it alone, and disagree
  total is (t1 + (t2 + t3))
  6000 descriptions were possible, 1 survived, support 0.9998
  it ran out of room before writing every description it could,
  so that share is over the part it searched, not over all of them
  it was seen across the whole range it is being used on

TWO. Rectangles: two sides, a colour code, and the space inside.
  it was given 3 readings and 8 situations, and told nothing else
    side_a   matters: situations 0 and 2 differ in it alone, and disagree
    side_b   matters: situations 0 and 3 differ in it alone, and disagree
    tint     silent: changed on its own and nothing followed
  area is (side_a * side_b)
  6000 descriptions were possible, 6 survived, support 0.9990
  it ran out of room before writing every description it could,
  so that share is over the part it searched, not over all of them
  another account fits just as well: (side_a * max(side_b, tint))
  used outside what it saw, so it is held as a guess

THREE. Something moving: how fast, for how long, and how far it got.
  it was given 2 readings and 7 situations, and told nothing else
    pace     matters: situations 0 and 1 differ in it alone, and disagree
    span     matters: situations 0 and 2 differ in it alone, and disagree
  gone is (pace * span)
  6000 descriptions were possible, 4 survived, support 0.9993
  it ran out of room before writing every description it could,
  so that share is over the part it searched, not over all of them
  another account fits just as well: (pace * max(span, 2))
  used outside what it saw, so it is held as a guess

The two above have nothing to do with each other.
  it wrote the same form for both: (side_a * side_b) and (pace * span)
  that is the analogy, and no one built an analogy step:
  both formulations were found by elimination and came out alike

FOUR. Three slots and a bell. Nobody says what makes it ring.
  it was given 3 readings and 6 situations, and told nothing else
    s1       matters: situations 2 and 3 differ in it alone, and disagree
    s2       matters: situations 1 and 3 differ in it alone, and disagree
    s3       matters: situations 1 and 4 differ in it alone, and disagree
  it named a quantity of its own: q1 = (s1 + (s2 + s3))
  rang is (q1 >= 2)
  3148 descriptions were possible, 8 survived, support 0.9975
  it ran out of room before writing every description it could,
  so that share is over the part it searched, not over all of them
  another account fits just as well: ((s1 * (s2 + s3)) >= 1)
  it was seen across the whole range it is being used on

Now it is asked about the world it just described for itself.
  the description it wrote, now the thing it thinks in
  a case it never saw, two of the other slots filled: rang = 1 (derived)
  told only that it stayed silent with the third slot filled,
  it works backwards: the other two hold 0 (derived)

FIVE. A pile shared out, and a count that goes round in cycles.
  each is (pile / parts)
  left is (n % 3)
  nobody mentioned division or remainders. They are just short
  descriptions, and short descriptions are what survives

SIX. A table with no outcome column at all. What is always true here?
  out of 223 statements it could make, 2 stand on their own:
    ((a + b) == 10)
    ((b % 2) <= 0)
  everything true of any two numbers at all was thrown out as saying
  nothing, and everything the others already force was thrown out too.
  the engine proved both of those, so this is what it knows, not a list

SEVEN. Numbers in a row, with no hint that order means anything.
  1 1 2 3 5 8 13 21   next is (prev + prev2)
  3 6 12 24 48 96     next is (prev + prev)
  growth and recurrence are not a separate faculty either

What this closes.
  the description was the last place a person was still doing the work.
  it is now found the same way everything else is: by elimination.
