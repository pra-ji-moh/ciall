  AUROC separating them       : 0.946   (0.5 = cannot tell at all)

  The model is confident in a region it has never seen. It has no
  channel for "no grounds", so it extrapolates and asserts.
  It does tell them apart, but BACKWARDS: more confident with no
  grounds than on a coin flip. Abstaining on low confidence would
  refuse the ambiguous cases, which it should answer, and answer
  the no-grounds ones, which it should refuse.
  Whatever replaces this has to abstain on no grounds and answer,
  uncertainly, on the ambiguous class. Abstaining there is wrong.
